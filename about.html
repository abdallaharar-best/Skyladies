Wellness, elevated

Sky Ladies Club is a private women-only destination created for relaxation, movement, and meaningful moments above the city. Located on the 60th floor of Al Nisr Plaza Tower in Sharjah, the club brings together a private rooftop swimming pool, Sky View Café, Reformer Pilates Studio, and Mind & Body Studio all within one inspiring space designed exclusively for women.

More than just a place to unwind, Sky Ladies Club is a space to spend the day your way. Swim beneath the open sky, enjoy your favourite drink with a view, move your body, recharge, or simply slow down and enjoy time with friends. With privacy, comfort, and community at its heart, every detail has been thoughtfully designed to create a place where women can truly feel at ease.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sky Ladies Club exclusively for ladies?

Yes. Sky Ladies Club is an exclusively ladies-only club, and all members of our team are female.

Is the swimming pool completely private?

Yes. Our rooftop swimming pool is 100% private and cannot be viewed from surrounding buildings. Located at the top of the tallest building in Sharjah, it offers a comfortable and private environment exclusively for women.

Are children allowed?

Yes. Children are welcome at Sky Ladies Club. For the privacy and comfort of our guests, boys above the age of 5 are not permitted inside the club. Children must remain supervised by a responsible adult at all times.

What is included with my booking?

Each guest booking includes one sunbed and one complimentary towel. Additional towels are available to rent for an extra charge.

What facilities are available?

Our facilities include showers, changing rooms, washrooms, and lockers. Lockers are available to rent for an additional charge.

Can I bring outside food or drinks?

Outside food, drinks, and snacks are not permitted. A selection of food, snacks, and beverages is available to purchase from our café.

Can I celebrate a special occasion?

Absolutely. Sky Ladies Club welcomes celebrating special occasions. If you would like to arrange a celebration or bring a cake, please contact our team in advance to discuss the available options.

Is parking available?

Sky Ladies Club does not have dedicated parking. However, paid hourly parking is available around the building for your convenience.
