Wellness, elevated

Sky Ladies Club is a private women-only destination created for relaxation, movement, and meaningful moments above the city. Located on the 60th floor of Al Nisr Plaza Tower in Sharjah, the club brings together a private rooftop swimming pool, Sky View Café, Reformer Pilates Studio, and Mind & Body Studio all within one inspiring space designed exclusively for women.

More than just a place to unwind, Sky Ladies Club is a space to spend the day your way. Swim beneath the open sky, enjoy your favourite drink with a view, move your body, recharge, or simply slow down and enjoy time with friends. With privacy, comfort, and community at its heart, every detail has been thoughtfully designed to create a place where women can truly feel at ease.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sky Ladies Club exclusively for ladies?

Yes. Sky Ladies Club is an exclusively ladies-only club, and all members of our team are female.

Is the swimming pool completely private?

Our rooftop pool is a fully private environment exclusively for women.

Are children allowed?

Please message us and our team will confirm the details for your visit.

Is there a swimming pool for children?

Yes. The rooftop includes a dedicated children’s pool alongside the main swimming pool.

What should I wear in the swimming pool?

Please message us and our team will share the pool dress guidance.
