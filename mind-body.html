Mind & Body

Find your balance at the Mind & Body Studio at Sky Ladies Club, a dedicated ladies-only wellness and fitness space in Sharjah. Designed to bring movement, relaxation and mindful exercise together, our multi-purpose studio hosts a variety of group classes, including yoga, stretching and other mind and body activities suitable for different fitness levels and interests.

Whether you're looking to improve flexibility, build strength, release tension or simply take time for yourself, our classes offer an opportunity to focus on your physical and mental well-being. From mindful yoga sessions to guided stretching and movement-based activities, each class provides a welcoming environment to move at your own pace and make wellness part of your routine.

Located in Al Majaz 3, Sharjah, within Sky Ladies Club, the Mind & Body Studio offers women a dedicated space to connect, recharge and stay active. With a changing weekly class schedule, there's always an opportunity to discover new activities, explore different fitness practices and enjoy a balanced approach to wellness.
