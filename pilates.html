Reformer Pilates Studio

Discover the benefits of Reformer Pilates at Sky Ladies Club, home to a dedicated ladies-only Reformer Pilates studio in Sharjah. Designed to support women at different fitness levels, our instructor-led Pilates classes combine controlled movements with professional reformer equipment to help improve strength, flexibility, mobility, balance and posture.

Whether you're new to Pilates or looking to build on your existing practice, our classes offer a supportive environment where you can focus on movement, technique and overall physical well-being. Each session is guided by an instructor, with exercises designed to engage the core, strengthen muscles, improve body alignment and develop greater body awareness.

Located in Al Majaz 3, Sharjah, our Reformer Pilates Studio offers women a dedicated space to move, build strength and enjoy a focused fitness experience. Whether your goal is to improve flexibility, develop core strength, support better posture or complement your existing workout routine, Sky Ladies Club provides a welcoming setting to make Pilates part of your lifestyle.
